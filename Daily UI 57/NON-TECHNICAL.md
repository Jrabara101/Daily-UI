# 🎬 CineFlow — Pro-Grade Video Player

**CineFlow** is a modern, feature-rich custom video player built to deliver a premium, cinema-like experience directly inside your web browser. Think of it as a custom-tailored, supercharged version of YouTube or Netflix's video players, built from the ground up to feel fast, responsive, and intuitive.

Here is a simple, non-technical overview of what CineFlow is, what it does, and how it makes watching videos online better.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Experience

### 📍 Interactive Video Chapters
Just like a book has chapters, CineFlow divides videos into visual sections on the progress bar.
* **Easy Navigation**: You can see exactly where one section ends and another begins.
* **Chapter Previews**: Hovering over a segment shows you the title of that chapter, allowing you to jump straight to the topic you care about.

### 🔍 Smart Preview Hovering (Scrubbing)
* **Visual Search**: When you hover your mouse cursor over the progress bar, you get a tiny popup thumbnail showing what is happening in the video at that exact second. This makes finding your favorite scene or skipping past an intro incredibly easy.

### 📱 Intuitive Gestures & Shortcuts
Watching videos should be effortless. CineFlow includes smart touch and mouse gestures:
* **Quick Skip**: Double-tap or double-click the right side of the screen to skip forward 10 seconds (or the left side to go back).
* **Easy Volume**: Swipe up and down or scroll to raise and lower the volume.
* **Pinch-to-Zoom**: Pinch on a touch device or zoom in/out to stretch the video to fill your screen.

### 🎭 Theater Mode (Cinema View)
* **Focus on the Show**: With a single click, you can switch to "Theater Mode." The video expands to fill more of your screen, the rest of the website dims, and the layout moves with a smooth transition, giving you a focused, movie-theater feel.

### ⏸️ Smart Auto-Pause
* **Never Miss a Moment**: If you switch to another tab, look at another window, or minimize the browser, CineFlow automatically pauses the video for you. When you return to the tab, the video is ready to resume right where you left off.

### 🖼️ Picture-in-Picture (Floating Player)
* **Watch While You Work**: You can pop the video out into a floating window that sits on top of all your other applications. This allows you to watch a tutorial or movie while typing notes, checking emails, or working in another app.

### 📊 Real-Time Buffer Loading
* **Connection Health**: A subtle indicator shows you exactly how much of the video has finished preloading in the background, so you know if your internet connection is keeping up before you hit play.

---

## 🎬 How to Use It

1. **Load a Video**: You can click one of the quick sample buttons (like the Big Buck Bunny cartoon) or paste in your own video link (both standard video files and live streams are supported).
2. **Interact with the Controls**: Enjoy custom-designed play, pause, volume, chapter, picture-in-picture, and screen-sizing controls.
3. **Change Chapters**: Edit the chapter details list on the left to instantly update the video's segments.
